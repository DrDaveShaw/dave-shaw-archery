About Dave

12 years building better archers

I'm a performance archery coach based in Yorkshire, working with junior and senior recurve and compound archers competing at regional and national level. My coaching is athlete-centred: I care as much about the person behind the bow as I do about the score on the target.

I coach at York Archers and Valley Bowmen of Huddersfield, and have guided athletes through the NTDP and into National Age Group (NAG) academy places — including national age-group champions. Alongside coaching, I'm a senior research fellow, and bring the same intellectual rigour to athlete development that I apply to my research.

In 2025 I delivered workshops on non-linear pedagogy in archery coaching at Northern Counties and Derbyshire coaching conferences — sharing evidence-based methods I use every session.

  • AGB County Coach (2019) · AGB Level 2 Coach (2016)
  • UK Coaching Focus Coach (2023)
  • NTDP Coach, three cohorts (2023–2025)
  • Mental Health Awareness for Sport & Physical Activity (2026)
  • Non-Linear Pedagogy in Archery — conference speaker (2025)
My approach

How I coach

I

Athlete-centred

You own your development. Constraints-led coaching and non-linear pedagogy mean you discover solutions, not just follow instructions.

II

Psychologically safe

High standards don't require a harsh environment. Mistakes are data. I build the trust that lets athletes take the risks that lead to breakthroughs.

III

Holistically integrated

GCSEs, A-levels, and work are part of the plan. Flexible programmes keep athletes healthy and progressing year-round.

IV

Technically rigorous

Rooted in the AGB technical framework, with considered adaptations for individual biomechanics, maturity, and injury history.
